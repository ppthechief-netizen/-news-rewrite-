10th August 2026 - (Hong Kong) A security union has praised a Bel-Air guard who charged unarmed at a knifeman this morning in Pok Fu Lam after a 61-year-old colleague surnamed Siu fell while trying to stop the attack on a domestic helper and estate staff.

The Hong Kong Building Management Employees General Union said the second officer put himself between the attacker and his injured mate, forced the suspect back and talked him toward calm — an act it called exemplary professional duty and selfless comradeship under extreme threat.

The union urged the property manager to offer concrete recognition such as a special bonus or promotion, to cover the injured guard's medical bills in full and to deliver statutory compensation under the Employees' Compensation Ordinance without delay.

It also called for professional post-trauma counselling for every guard who saw or helped stop the assailant, warning that life-threatening knife confrontations leave deep psychological strain as well as physical risk long after the blade is secured.

The incident, the union added, again showed frontline officers' "equipment weakness": they had to help a fallen colleague empty-handed. While the first duty is to call police and evacuate when safe, it asked management to station lawful defensive gear at block lobbies or control rooms, strengthen crisis drills, teach retreat tactics and publish clear emergency playbooks so staff are not left waiting for officers with nothing but their bodies between residents and a weapon.

Estate managers have yet to announce any bonus or counselling package in response to the appeal, as investigators continue to examine the morning attack at Bel-Air on Victoria Road and the sequence captured on circulating video from the scene this morning. Residents praised the second guard online for putting colleagues and tenants ahead of his own safety.
