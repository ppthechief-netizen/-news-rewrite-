10th August 2026 - (Hong Kong) All four restaurant floors inside the century-old Woo Cheong Pawn Shop at 60-66 Johnston Road, Wan Chai, appear to have gone dark, with online listings marking every outlet closed and reporters finding the interior lights off today.

The cluster covers ground-floor Sophia Loren Pizzeria, first-floor Casa Sophia Loren, second-floor cocktail lounge The Stage and rooftop bar Pellicola Rooftop. Built in 1888 as four linked Guangzhou-style tong lau with verandahs, the landmark was restored by the Urban Renewal Authority after a HK$15 million-plus outlay in 2007 and leased as upscale dining.

Tenancy has been brittle for years despite the intact historic shell. Western restaurant The Pawn closed in 2021; Woo Cheong Restaurant lasted about two years before exiting at the end of 2023. The Sophia Loren brand then took all four floors — pizza, fine Italian, cocktails and a rooftop bar — yet Casa Sophia Loren on the first floor shut under pressure in February.

Management later said it was concentrating resources and in May denied an exit, urging customers to trust their taste buds and stay put. Within months, however, the pizza shop, first-floor restaurant and second-floor bar all showed as closed on search platforms, and the official Instagram quietly posted "Closed".

The blank windows revive questions about whether URA heritage-dining conversions can hold tenants when rents, tourism swings and operating costs keep flipping concepts. Woo Cheong Pawn Shop remains an architectural draw on Johnston Road; filling its kitchens again is the harder conservation test after repeated turnovers.

Passers-by still photograph the verandahs, but without lit dining rooms the restored tong lau risks becoming another Wan Chai shell — conserved brick with no evening trade behind the glass after dark, and few signs of a fresh operator lining up.
