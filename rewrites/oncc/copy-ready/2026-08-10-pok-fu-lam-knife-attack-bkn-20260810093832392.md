10th August 2026 - (Hong Kong) A stabbing broke out in Pok Fu Lam this morning after officers took multiple reports at 9.24am of fighting noises from a flat upstairs at Bel-Air, 550 Victoria Road.

Police arriving on scene arrested a bare-chested, barefoot non-Chinese man on the spot and seized a sharp knife. An overseas domestic helper suffered knife wounds to her back and neck, a security guard grazed a hand in a struggle, and a woman jogging past was also hurt. All three were conscious when ambulances took them to hospital for treatment.

Early inquiries suggest the arrested man has a psychiatric history and lives in a flat in Block 47 at the estate. Officers believe he may have suffered an episode, took a blade from home and ran into the street, with a foreign male friend trailing him through the grounds. He allegedly swung the knife at the helper without warning, then tried to attack a guard, who fell and scraped a hand during the tangle before colleagues intervened.

The man's mother reached the scene after hearing the news and broke down in tears when she saw her son in custody. During the search officers found a cat with a head injury inside his flat, and staff from the Society for the Prevention of Cruelty to Animals were called to take the animal away for veterinary care.

Online video circulating after the incident shows a shirtless, barefoot man chasing another foreign man with a knife, then suddenly turning on a nearby guard who collapses in the struggle. A woman passer-by screams while a second guard steps in to stop further blows. The attacker then appears to calm and stands still as colleagues help the fallen guard to his feet, before the clip ends. Police continue to investigate the full sequence, the bloodstained blade recovered at the scene, and the man's medical background.
