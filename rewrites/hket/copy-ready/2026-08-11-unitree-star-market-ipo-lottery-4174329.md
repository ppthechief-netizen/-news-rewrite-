11th August 2026 - (Hong Kong) Unitree Robotics, trading prospectively under Shanghai code 688836, has disclosed an extremely tight online allotment for its STAR Market initial public offering after pricing the issue at 150.8 yuan a share.

According to an announcement published on the Shanghai Stock Exchange on the evening of 10th August, the online tranche drew about 9.7846 million valid subscription accounts seeking 53.637 billion shares. The preliminary online lottery rate was 0.01206444 per cent, and the final rate settled at 0.01809759 per cent — far below the 0.02 per cent to 0.03 per cent band many market participants had been discussing ahead of the draw.

That final hit rate also sits well under the online allotment recorded for earlier STAR Market name Changxin Memory Technology, which cleared at about 0.471 per cent. The contrast underscores how crowded the Unitree book was relative to a recent high-profile semiconductor float on the same board, leaving retail applicants with only a slim mathematical chance of receiving stock.

In practical terms, fewer than two in every 10,000 online lots cleared at the final rate, even after the step-up from the still-tighter preliminary figure. With more than 53 billion shares notionally sought against a far smaller online supply, the lottery result will be read as evidence of intense speculative and thematic demand for listed humanoid and quadruped robotics exposure on the A-share market.

Pricing at 150.8 yuan a share fixes the cash outlay for every successful applicant and frames secondary-market expectations once trading begins. Because lottery odds are this low, most online subscribers will simply have cash returned after the freeze period, while the minority who are allotted stock will watch the opening premium or discount closely as the first real mark on the IPO price.

For Hong Kong and mainland investors who follow cross-border technology themes, Unitree's allotment print matters less as a day-trading tip than as a gauge of how aggressively domestic liquidity is chasing robotics listings. When a STAR Market IPO clears below even a 0.02 per cent lottery band, it signals that primary-market demand remains concentrated in a handful of narrative names rather than spreading evenly across new issues.

The company will now move through the remaining post-lottery timetable toward listing on the Sci-Tech Innovation Board. Until the stock debuts, the 150.8 yuan issue price, the 9.78 million valid online accounts and the 0.0181 per cent final hit rate stand as the hard numbers from the Shanghai exchange disclosure that define this China finance story.
