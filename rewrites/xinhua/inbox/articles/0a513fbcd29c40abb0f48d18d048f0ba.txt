TITLE: 7.5-magnitude quake hits Colombia: CENC
PUBLISHED: 2026-08-10 21:13:00
URL: https://english.news.cn/20260810/0a513fbcd29c40abb0f48d18d048f0ba/c.html

BEIJING, Aug. 10 (Xinhua) -- A 7.5-magnitude earthquake jolted Colombia at 8:34 p.m. on Monday (Beijing Time), according to the China Earthquake Networks Center (CENC).

The epicenter was monitored at 4.85 degrees north latitude and 76.15 degrees west longitude. The earthquake struck at a depth of 80 km, the center said. ■
