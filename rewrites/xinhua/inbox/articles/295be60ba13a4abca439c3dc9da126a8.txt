TITLE: 6.8-magnitude quake hits Colombia -- GFZ
PUBLISHED: 2026-08-10 20:44:45
URL: https://english.news.cn/20260810/295be60ba13a4abca439c3dc9da126a8/c.html

BEIJING, Aug. 10 (Xinhua) -- An earthquake with a magnitude of 6.8 jolted Colombia at 1234 GMT on Monday, the GFZ German Research Centre for Geosciences said.

The epicenter, with a depth of 93.7 km, was initially determined to be at 4.86 degrees north latitude and 76.19 degrees west longitude. ■
