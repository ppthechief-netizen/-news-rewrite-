10th August 2026 - (Beijing) China's leading digital publishing expo will open in late August with its first dedicated exhibition area on the integration of traditional Chinese culture and online games, organisers said on Monday.

The 16th China International Digital Publishing Expo runs from 27th to 29th August in Jinan, capital of Shandong Province, according to the organising committee. Planners are using the new gaming section to showcase how studios adapt classics, folklore and heritage motifs into interactive entertainment.

Homegrown titles such as Black Myth: Wukong have, in recent years, underlined technological advances in China's gaming industry while presenting traditional culture through creative transformation. Organisers cast that success as proof that culture-led games can travel both commercially and culturally.

In 2025, China's domestic gaming market generated actual sales revenue of more than 350 billion yuan (about 52 billion US dollars), up 7.68 per cent year on year, with users reaching 683 million, an increase of 1.35 per cent. Those figures give the expo a strong industrial backdrop as publishers, platforms and regulators gather in Jinan.

More than 500 exhibitors are expected, including delegations from 20 provincial-level regions across China and overseas participants from more than 30 countries and regions. The Chinese Academy of Press and Publication, a co-host, will unveil interim achievements in drafting industry standards for artificial-intelligence applications during the event.

The programme includes one main forum, six thematic forums and more than 80 supporting activities covering digital publishing, human-machine collaboration, interdisciplinary construction and global academic communication. First held in 2005, the expo has become a major platform for the digital publishing industry's development.

By pairing a culture-and-games pavilion with AI standards work and a packed forum slate, organisers are signalling that China's digital content agenda now spans books, interactive entertainment and machine-assisted publishing in one trade-show week. Exhibitors and overseas visitors will arrive at the end of August to see how that mix is staged on the Jinan show floor.
