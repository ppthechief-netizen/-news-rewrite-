10th August 2026 - (Beijing) A 7.5-magnitude earthquake jolted Colombia at 8.34pm on Monday, Beijing time, according to the China Earthquake Networks Center.

The centre timed the shock for late Monday evening on the Beijing clock, fixing the origin at a magnitude of 7.5 rather than a moderate tremor. A reading at that level marks a major seismic event in the global catalogue and explains why CENC issued a prompt public bulletin from Beijing even though the rupture lay across the Pacific in South America.

The epicentre was monitored at 4.85 degrees north latitude and 76.15 degrees west longitude. Those coordinates place the initial location in western Colombia's latitude-longitude grid as tracked by Chinese instruments, giving responders and analysts a precise point from which to assess distance to towns, fault systems and earlier felt reports once local agencies publish their own assessments.

The earthquake struck at a depth of 80 km, the centre said. An intermediate depth of 80 kilometres means the rupture began well below the shallow crust where many damaging quakes nucleate, a parameter that can influence how strongly shaking is felt at the surface and how widely it radiates. CENC's bulletin paired that depth figure with the 7.5 magnitude and the epicentral coordinates rather than listing casualties, damage or aftershock counts.

Taken together, Monday's CENC notice supplies a compact instrumental snapshot: magnitude 7.5, origin time 8.34pm Beijing time, epicentre at 4.85 degrees north and 76.15 degrees west, and a focal depth of 80 km. No further details on intensity, fatalities or structural damage appeared in the centre's account, which confined itself to the timing, size and geometry of the rupture as measured from Beijing.

The bulletin therefore stands as an early international reading of a powerful Colombian earthquake, issued by China's national earthquake networks centre for a South American event large enough to register clearly on its systems. Until Colombian or other agencies release ground-truth reports, the CENC figures — 7.5 magnitude, 8.34pm Beijing time, 80 km depth and the stated epicentral coordinates — remain the verified parameters carried in Monday's wire.
