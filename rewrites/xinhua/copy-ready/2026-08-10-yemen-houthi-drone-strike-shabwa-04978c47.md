10th August 2026 - (Aden) Three pro-government Yemeni soldiers were killed and nine others wounded on Monday when Houthi drones struck positions in the southeastern oil-rich province of Shabwa, a military official said, amid a sharper escalation in the war-torn country.

The official, speaking on condition of anonymity, said Houthi forces used drones against the 3rd Brigade of the pro-government Shabwa Defense Forces in an area near the Shabwa-Marib provincial border. There was no immediate comment from the Houthi group.

The Houthis have recently intensified missile and drone attacks on government-held areas inside Yemen and on targets in Saudi Arabia, a main ally of the internationally recognised Yemeni government. On Sunday the group launched a wave of attacks on Mocha, a government-held port city on Yemen's western coast.

In July the group announced a maritime ban on Saudi shipping and has since attacked Saudi tankers in the Red Sea as well as oil facilities on Saudi soil. Monday's Shabwa strike therefore sits inside a broader pattern of air and sea pressure rather than an isolated border skirmish.

Yemen has been mired in conflict since late 2014, when the Houthis seized much of northern Yemen, including the capital Sanaa. A Saudi-led coalition intervened in 2015 in support of the internationally recognised government, opening a multi-front war that has repeatedly drawn in Red Sea shipping lanes and neighbouring energy infrastructure.

A United Nations-brokered truce that took effect in April 2022 expired six months later without a formal extension, but it ushered in a period of significantly reduced hostilities that largely held until the recent escalation. The return of deadly drone raids on brigade positions, port assaults and strikes linked to Saudi shipping marks a clear break from that quieter phase.

Local commanders and civilians in Shabwa and Marib will now watch whether further drone waves follow along the provincial seam, while Aden-based authorities tally the wounded from Monday's attack. With three soldiers dead, nine hurt and no Houthi claim yet issued, the immediate military picture remains incomplete even as the wider escalation is already unmistakable.
