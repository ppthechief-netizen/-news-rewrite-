10th August 2026 - (Dhaka) At least 16 Bangladeshi nationals have been killed in a devastating fire in Saudi Arabia, a ministry notification said in Dhaka on Monday.

According to the notification issued by the country's Expatriates' Welfare and Overseas Employment Ministry, the fire broke out at a sofa factory in Riyadh, the capital of Saudi Arabia, on Sunday, killing 16 people on the spot.

The notification quoted witnesses as saying all the victims were from Bangladesh. The ministry issued its account from Dhaka on Monday, confirming the toll and the location of the blaze at the Riyadh sofa factory where the 16 deaths occurred on Sunday.

Bangladesh's Ministry of Foreign Affairs said Bangladesh embassy officials in Riyadh are working in close coordination with relevant Saudi authorities to identify the deceased and complete necessary formalities concerning the bodies.

The devastating fire in Saudi Arabia killed the 16 Bangladeshi nationals on the spot at the Riyadh factory on Sunday, the expatriates ministry said. Witnesses quoted in its Monday notification told officials that every victim was from Bangladesh.

The embassy's role in Riyadh, as described by the foreign ministry, centres on liaison with Saudi authorities over the victims killed at the factory fire. Officials are tasked with identifying the deceased Bangladeshi nationals and handling the necessary steps regarding their bodies in coordination with the Saudi side.

Bangladeshi Prime Minister Tarique Rahman on Monday expressed deep grief over the tragic death of the Bangladeshi nationals. His remarks came as Dhaka's expatriates ministry and foreign ministry both issued statements about the fire that killed 16 Bangladeshi workers at the Riyadh sofa factory on Sunday.

The ministry notification, the foreign ministry's account of embassy coordination in the Saudi capital, and the prime minister's expression of grief together formed Bangladesh's official response on Monday to the fatal blaze overseas.
