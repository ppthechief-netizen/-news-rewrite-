10th August 2026 - (Bogota) At least 18 people died in Pereira in western Colombia after a magnitude-7.4 earthquake, city mayor Mauricio Salazar said on Monday, sharpening the human toll from the powerful western shock.

Salazar gave the figure in a radio interview as rescue and damage assessments continued in the coffee-region city. The early count covers fatalities inside Pereira and does not yet amount to a full national toll for every affected municipality along the Pacific and Andean belt shaken by the same event.

A magnitude near 7.4 places the tremor among the stronger shocks to strike Colombia in recent years, capable of toppling vulnerable masonry, triggering landslides on steep slopes and injuring people caught indoors when buildings cracked or collapsed. Pereira, a major urban centre in the west, concentrates apartment blocks, commercial streets and hillside neighbourhoods that can fare unevenly when the ground moves violently.

Emergency crews typically face a race through the first night and following day: pulling survivors from debris, treating the injured at hospitals already under pressure, and checking bridges, schools and mid-rise flats for structural failure. Families searching for missing relatives add to the demand on hotlines and neighbourhood wardens while aftershocks remain a risk.

Earlier Monday advisories from international and Chinese seismological centres had already flagged a major western Colombia earthquake, with magnitude estimates in the high sixes to mid-sevens and an epicentre near the Pacific margin. Salazar's Pereira update is the clearest local confirmation so far that the shock has killed on a significant scale inside a named city, rather than leaving only provincial reports of casualties without numbers.

Authorities have not yet published in this bulletin a full list of the dead, a hospital injury tally for Pereira, or a building-by-building damage map. Those details usually follow once civil defence teams finish sector sweeps and mayors reconcile reports from police, firefighters and health officials.

For now, the confirmed picture from the mayor is stark and specific: at least 18 dead in Pereira after a magnitude-7.4 earthquake on Monday. Further revisions to the toll remain likely as search teams work through damaged districts and as other western towns report their own losses from the same widespread shaking.
