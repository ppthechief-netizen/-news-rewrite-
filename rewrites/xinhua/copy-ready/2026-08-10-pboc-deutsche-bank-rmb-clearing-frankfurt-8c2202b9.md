10th August 2026 - (Beijing) The People's Bank of China, the country's central bank, announced on Monday that it has decided to authorise Deutsche Bank to serve as the renminbi clearing bank in Frankfurt, Germany.

The PBOC said the decision was made in accordance with a memorandum of understanding between the central bank and Deutsche Bank. That agreement provided the formal basis for Monday's authorisation, linking the lender's new clearing role directly to prior arrangements between the two institutions rather than to an ad hoc designation. The central bank did not release further details of the memorandum in its announcement.

Authorising a clearing bank in Frankfurt extends China's offshore renminbi infrastructure into one of Europe's principal financial centres. Frankfurt hosts a dense cluster of banks, corporates and market infrastructure, making it a natural node for local renminbi settlement if Chinese policy makers want yuan use to deepen in continental Europe. A clearing bank typically settles cross-border yuan transactions locally, helping businesses and financial institutions convert, transfer and manage renminbi without routing every payment through onshore channels. The PBOC's announcement did not spell out operational details, but the designation marks Deutsche Bank as the institution responsible for that function in the German city.

For Deutsche Bank, the appointment places it at the centre of yuan liquidity and settlement services for clients active in or connected to Frankfurt. For the PBOC, naming a clearing bank abroad is a standard step in building usable offshore renminbi rails that complement China's domestic currency management. The central bank framed the move as an implementation decision flowing from the memorandum of understanding already in place with the German lender.

Monday's statement was brief and did not disclose further terms of the memorandum, transaction volumes, or timelines for full operational rollout. What it made explicit was the policy outcome: Deutsche Bank is now the PBOC-authorised renminbi clearing bank for Frankfurt, Germany, under the agreed framework between the central bank and the bank. The announcement therefore confirms both the city and the institution entrusted with clearing, while leaving operational specifics to later implementation under the memorandum of understanding.
