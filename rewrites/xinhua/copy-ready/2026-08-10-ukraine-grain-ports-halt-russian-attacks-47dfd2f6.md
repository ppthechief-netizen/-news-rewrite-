10th August 2026 - (Kyiv) Three Ukrainian deep-water ports handling 90 per cent of the country's agricultural exports have halted operations after Russian attacks, Ukraine's Agriculture Ministry said on Monday.

The ministry said Russia has carried out at least 180 attacks on port infrastructure in the Greater Odesa area since the beginning of 2026. Those strikes have targeted the facilities through which the bulk of Ukraine's farm shipments reach international markets, forcing a stop to operations at the three affected harbours. Agriculture officials linked the halt directly to the latest round of attacks rather than to a voluntary closure.

Ukraine's grain exports in the 2026-2027 marketing year could fall to 29.6 million tonnes, down 54 per cent from an earlier forecast, the UNN news agency reported. The revised outlook underscores how damage and disruption at export gateways can quickly translate into lower shipment volumes even when fields produce usable crops. A cut of more than half from the prior projection points to a sharply narrower export window if the ports remain offline or damaged.

The Greater Odesa region incorporates the ports of Odesa, Chornomorsk and Pivdenny. Together they form the principal deep-water corridor for Ukrainian agricultural trade, which is why their simultaneous halt carries outsized weight for the national export programme. Because the three ports account for 90 per cent of agricultural exports by volume, any interruption there reverberates across the entire sector's ability to ship abroad.

With operations suspended at all three ports and port infrastructure under sustained attack since the start of the year, Ukrainian agricultural exporters face renewed uncertainty over how much grain can move by sea in the current marketing year. The ministry's tally of at least 180 strikes in the Greater Odesa area frames the shutdown as the latest escalation in a campaign directly aimed at export infrastructure. Farmers and traders must now plan around damaged berths, interrupted loading schedules and a forecast that already prices in a steep drop from earlier expectations.
