10th August 2026 - (Jakarta) Eight people were killed and nine others injured when a drag-racing car lost control and ploughed into spectators during a motorsport event in Kotamobagu, North Sulawesi, local police said on Monday.

The crash happened on Sunday at the Tidar Drag Race Championship 2026, which was staged on a public road without barriers or safety fencing to separate the track from the crowd. That layout left onlookers standing close to racing cars with little physical protection if a vehicle left the course.

Video circulating online showed a racing car swerving off the route about 300 metres from the starting line before striking people standing nearby. The footage quickly amplified public anger about how close fans had been allowed to stand during competitive runs on an ordinary roadway.

Local police said authorities were investigating the incident. Early statements did not name the driver, list the victims or say whether organisers had permits that matched the safety standards expected at sanctioned circuits. Those details are likely to dominate the inquiry as officers reconstruct speed, braking and crowd control.

The tragedy has sharpened concern over safety at amateur racing events held on Indonesian public roads, where spectators are often positioned beside the racing line without adequate shielding. Unlike purpose-built tracks with runoff zones, catch fences and stewarding, street-style drag strips can leave little margin when a car snaps sideways or suffers a mechanical failure.

Motorsport on open roads remains popular in parts of the archipelago because it is cheaper to stage and easier for local fans to attend, but Sunday's toll shows the cost when control is lost in a packed roadside gallery. Families of the dead and injured will press for answers on who approved the course layout and why barriers were absent.

Investigators are expected to examine organiser responsibilities, policing of the crowd line and whether similar events should face tighter rules or temporary bans while standards are reviewed. For now, the confirmed toll stands at eight killed and nine hurt, with the championship date and Kotamobagu location fixed in the official account of one of the weekend's deadliest spectator accidents.
