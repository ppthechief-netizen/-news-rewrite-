11th August 2026 - (Hong Kong) An Italian dining brand that occupied all four floors of Wan Chai's century-old Woo Cheong Pawn Shop landmark on Johnston Road has shut every restaurant on the site.

The cluster of four linked tong lau with verandahs was built between 1888 and the 1900s. The Urban Renewal Authority spent more than HK$15 million restoring it in 2007 before letting it as a high-end bar and restaurant.

Tenants have turned over often. Western restaurant The Pawn closed in 2021; Woo Cheong Restaurant lasted about two years and left at the end of 2023. Then the Italian brand co-founded by Oscar-winning actress Sophia Loren took the whole building for pizza, fine Italian dining and a music bar.

Fine-dining Casa Sophia Loren on the first floor lasted under two years and announced closure in February, saying it would refocus resources while the ground-floor pizzeria, second-floor lounge and rooftop bar would stay open.

In May the brand posted that the restaurant was "not going anywhere" and urged people to trust their taste buds, not rumours. Months later, Sophia Loren Pizzeria, Casa Sophia Loren and the lounge all show as closed on restaurant search sites, and the group's official social channel is marked "Closed".

The empty verandah block now marks another failed hospitality chapter at a heritage site that the Urban Renewal Authority once billed as a showcase for adaptive reuse on Johnston Road.

Locals who watched The Pawn and Woo Cheong Restaurant come and go say the latest full shutdown leaves one of Wan Chai's most photographed facades without a dining draw once again.

For a building restored at public expense to host destination dining, successive exits within five years underline how hard it remains to keep a heritage restaurant trade alive on busy Johnston Road.

Passers-by now find darkened windows where thin-crust pizza and rooftop drinks were marketed only months ago.
