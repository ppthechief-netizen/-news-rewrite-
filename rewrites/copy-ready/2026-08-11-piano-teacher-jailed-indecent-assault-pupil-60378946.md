11th August 2026 - (Hong Kong) A 78-year-old piano teacher who indecently assaulted a 16-year-old girl from a single-parent family during a lesson has been sentenced to one year in prison after a Kowloon City magistrate said he exploited her longing for a father figure and blurred the teacher-pupil boundary through messaging apps.

Ng Lap-wai, a piano teacher, was convicted after trial of one count of indecent assault in case KCCC900011/2026. The charge said that on 30th March 2025, at Kam Sing Piano on the second floor of Amoy Plaza Phase 3, 77 Ngau Tau Kok Road, Kowloon Bay, he indecently assaulted girl X. Magistrate Mr MOK Tze-chung, Andrew, sentenced him today at Kowloon City Magistrates' Courts.

Evidence at trial showed Ng had long messaged the pupil before the attack and once told her she could call him "Daddy", adding that he could not express his affection. Defence counsel said Ng did not wholly deny the allegations, accepted some contact was inappropriate and promised to be more careful, while a report assessed his risk of reoffending as low. Counsel also asked for mercy because his wife's death had hit him hard. The magistrate said a conviction after trial left no room for a plea discount.

Mr Mok stressed the more than 60-year age gap and Ng's role as her piano teacher, finding he used that position to offend repeatedly in a confined teaching room and breached trust. Although only one count was charged, the facts covered multiple touches. Through messaging software Ng expressed excessive concern and asked her to address him as "Daddy", exploiting her need for paternal care. By using technology to muddy the teacher-student line he lowered her guard and drew her closer until the relationship turned abusive, the court said, requiring a deterrent sentence of 12 months.

In a video-recorded interview X said she attended the shop for a lesson with Ng seated to her right. After about ten minutes he suddenly touched her leg; she glared and he withdrew, then tried to wrap both hands around her left foot. When she crossed her legs to stop him he reached into the gap between her thighs. He later massaged her shoulders, touched her collarbone, took her hand to his private parts and pressed his groin against her back. At the end of class he hugged her and said, "See you next week." She told her mother and the police were called.

X said she had studied with Ng for about a year and a half in an ordinary teacher-pupil relationship. Knowing she came from a single-parent home, he messaged that a teacher would always treat her as his child and care for her, and that when no one else was around she need only call him "Daddy". He had also asked her out during lessons; she declined, citing strict family rules. On her 16th birthday he wrote that he could not express a daddy's affection.

The one-year term underlines how courts treat sexual abuse in private music lessons when messaging is used to create a pseudo-family bond before physical contact begins. Ng's age, bereavement and low reoffending score did not outweigh the breach of trust against a teenage pupil who had sought piano tuition, not a father substitute.
