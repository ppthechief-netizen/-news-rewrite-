10th August 2026 - (Hong Kong) The government today (10th August) announced that it is launching the upgrade plan for Sham Shui Po's Bishop Hill, whose official name is Woh Chai Shan. Clearance of unlawful and unsafe structures, facilities or clutter will proceed in two sealed stages so that recreation and safety works can follow on a tidier hillside.

The first stage covers the hillside rest area at Tong Yin Street, running from the foot of the slope up to the summit. The second stage will cordon the stretch from the foot of the hill near Berwick Street up to the summit. The two phases are intended to strip out illegal and unsafe builds, equipment and miscellaneous objects that have accumulated on the government land.

The Home Affairs Department said on its social-media page today that the various upgrade packages are advancing at different speeds, and the government hopes the items can be completed from late August through September. On religious articles now placed on the hill, the department stressed that the government particularly respects such items and has already invited religious leaders to help handle them properly.

That help is to include assisting with solemn religious rites and inviting the articles to suitable places rather than clearing sacred objects in a casual way. The government will keep talking with different stakeholders throughout, it said, to ensure the whole "double upgrade" of facilities and safety runs smoothly and ultimately gives residents better, safer rest and recreation facilities on Woh Chai Shan.
