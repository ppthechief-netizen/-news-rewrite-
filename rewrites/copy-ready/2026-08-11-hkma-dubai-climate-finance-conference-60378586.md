11th August 2026 - (Hong Kong) The Hong Kong Monetary Authority and the Dubai Financial Services Authority announced yesterday that they will co-host their third Joint Climate Finance Conference in Hong Kong on 10th September, a flagship of their partnership to support climate finance across Asia and the Middle East.

This year's theme is "Driving Transition Amid Change", and the gathering will be a main event of Hong Kong Green Week 2026. Hong Kong Exchanges and Clearing Limited and Nasdaq Dubai are supporting institutions. Organisers say the programme will bring together corporate leaders, investors and policymakers to examine partnerships, financing tools and innovation needed for a credible green transition under global uncertainty.

The agenda is expected to mix keynote speeches, dialogues, thematic addresses and panel discussions focused on key and emerging fields in Hong Kong and Dubai, highlighting how the two financial centres can inject momentum into transition finance. The series has become a recurring bridge between Asian and Middle Eastern sustainable-finance communities since the two regulators launched the joint format.

HKMA chief executive Eddie Yue said that as global uncertainty challenges the green transition, major sustainable-finance hubs such as Hong Kong and Dubai should work together to channel capital toward practical solutions. He welcomed cross-regional dialogue and said he looked forward to turning discussion into real business and investment opportunities.

DFSA chief executive Mark Steward said the conference reflected the close partnership between the two authorities and offered a valuable platform to exchange forward-looking insights, strengthen cross-border cooperation and advance sustainable finance in both centres. His remarks echoed earlier editions that stressed the Asia–Middle East corridor as a route for climate capital and knowledge flows.

By placing the third edition in Hong Kong during Green Week, the hosts are signalling that transition finance is no longer a side conversation but a core market-building exercise for both hubs. Corporate treasurers, asset owners and policymakers are being invited to move from ambition statements to bankable structures — the partnerships, products and innovations Yue and Steward both framed as the point of the dialogue.

For Hong Kong the timing also underlines Green Week's role as a showcase week for ESG products, listing venues and regional deal-making, with Dubai's presence reinforcing a two-way corridor rather than a one-off conference tour. Market participants will watch whether the September sessions produce concrete pipelines after previous joint research and Dubai-hosted editions.
