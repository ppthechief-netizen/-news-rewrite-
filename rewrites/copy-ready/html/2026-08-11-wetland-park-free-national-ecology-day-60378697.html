11th August 2026 - (Hong Kong) Saturday, 15th August, is National Ecology Day, and the government with non-governmental groups will run special events and offers through August and September to draw the public outdoors.

Hong Kong Wetland Park will open free that Saturday and provide free public guided tours on site. The same day, recycling through the GREEN@COMMUNITY network, including smart recycling bins, will earn double GREEN$ points — food-waste recycling excepted.

The Agriculture, Fisheries and Conservation Department is staging ecology programmes so residents can meet local wildlife and Hong Kong's distinctive nature. Four guided tours will run at Shing Mun Country Park and the Lions Nature Education Centre; an evening session at Tai Po Kau Nature Reserve will share tips on watching fireflies; Long Valley Nature Park will offer free public guided tours; and several marine ecology tours are planned.

AFCD will also take a travelling exhibition on the thirtieth anniversary of marine parks to the Hong Kong Maritime Museum. More than 30 non-governmental organisations will join with free facility openings, guided walks, night tours, workshops and talks.

The Countryside Conservation Office under the Environment and Ecology Bureau will hold a National Ecology Day thematic exhibition from 1st to 6th September at Exhibition Corner, the indoor space on the ground floor of East Kowloon Cultural Centre, using interactive displays to raise awareness of nature and environmental protection.

On 5th September the office will host a sharing session and performance of Tan Dun's Sounddance Landscape, with harmonica and violin on stage portraying remote villages around Yan Chau Tong, alongside work by an internationally known landscape photographer on Daya Bay scenes from the same area.

Details of all National Ecology Day activities and offers are on the Countryside Conservation Office website, AFCD's Hong Kong Biodiversity Information Hub and the dedicated National Ecology Day page.
