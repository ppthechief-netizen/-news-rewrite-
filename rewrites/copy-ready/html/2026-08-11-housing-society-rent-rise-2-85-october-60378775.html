11th August 2026 - (Hong Kong) The Hong Kong Housing Society announced today that its executive committee has approved new rents for its rental housing estates for the 2026 to 2028 period, with charges rising 2.85 per cent from 1st October and the adjusted levels held until 30th September 2028.

After the increase, tenants in category A rental estates will on average pay about HK$73 more each month, while category B tenants will on average pay about HK$200 more. The society said that, as a self-financing non-profit body, it conducts a rent review every two years, weighing each estate's operating accounts and balancing running costs against residents' ability to pay.

The rise covers most of the society's rental estates. Published figures repeat the average monthly increases of about HK$73 for category A and about HK$200 for category B. Four estates that welcomed tenants within the past two years are excluded from this review: Yue Ying House in Aberdeen, Block 1 of Lok Kiu House at Hung Shui Kiu and Ha Tsuen, Lok Ling House in Fanling, and Block 1 of Hung Kok Court in Ngau Tau Kok.

The society pointed to the growing age of its estates and said it has spent heavily over the past two years upgrading facilities, pushing operating costs higher and leaving rental accounts in deficit. It expects operating losses on rental estate business to continue over the next two years, framing the modest increase as part of sustaining services across ageing stock while keeping charges below what a full cost-recovery exercise might require.

For tenants facing temporary financial hardship, the society runs a Rent Assistance Programme. Eligible applicants who succeed can receive a reduction of one quarter or one half of their rent to ease living pressure while the new schedule remains in force. Officials urged anyone struggling with the October adjustment to enquire early rather than fall into arrears.
