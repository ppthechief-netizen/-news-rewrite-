11th August 2026 - (Hong Kong) Police are appealing for information on Tam Hing-man, 72, who vanished after leaving his home in Pek Tin Street, Tai Wai, on the morning of 8th August.

Mr Tam is about 1.73 metres tall, weighs about 65 kilograms, of medium build with a square face, yellow complexion and short white hair. He was last seen in a blue top, black shorts, orange-and-white shoes and carrying a black backpack.

His family said he loves hiking and has thirty years' experience. About 9am on 8th August he set out from Chun Yeung Estate in Fo Tan with friends but lost the group near Sheung Kwai Chung New Village and is suspected to have suffered heatstroke.

About 8pm that evening relatives briefly reached him by phone; he said he was walking downhill and could almost see Wong Chuk Yeung Village, then the line died, believed because his battery failed. His last phone signal was estimated near The Arles in Fo Tan.

The volunteer group Hong Kong Watchers has been searching Kau To Shan. Anyone with news should call 3661 1176 or 5562 1342, email the New Territories South Regional Missing Persons Unit, or contact any police station.

Relatives fear dehydration in the extreme heat after he became separated from the hiking party. Searchers ask walkers in the Fo Tan and Kau To hills to check remote paths, stream beds and shaded rest spots for a man matching Mr Tam's clothing and white hair.

Hill parties are reminded that even experienced hikers can lose bearings when heatstroke sets in, and that a final phone call describing Wong Chuk Yeung Village below can still leave a long walk to a road if batteries fail before rescuers arrive.

Anyone who hiked the Fo Tan–Kau To corridor on 8th August or later and remembers seeing an older man in a blue top with a black backpack is urged to replay their route timing and contact the missing persons unit without delay.
