10th August 2026 - (Hong Kong) Tam Hing-man, 72, went hiking with friends in Fo Tan on Saturday morning, 8th August, later went missing, and remains unaccounted for today, 10th August, as his family urgently seeks public help.

His daughter said he normally loves hiking and has about thirty years of experience. At about 9am on the day he set out with friends from Chun Yeung Estate in Fo Tan, but lost the group near Sheung Kwai Ti New Village and then dropped out of contact.

About 8pm that evening the family briefly reached him by phone; he said he was heading downhill and would soon see Wong Chuk Yeung Village, yet the line then failed, apparently after the battery died, and there has been no word since. She said the extreme heat left her worried he was trapped on the hillside through dehydration or heatstroke. The last estimated phone signal was near The Great Hill in Fo Tan.

Fire Services and other rescuers are still searching that area and have set assembly points at Au Pui Wan Street and Tat Yip Lane in Fo Tan, with Government Flying Service helicopters also supporting the hunt. The civic group Hong Kong Watchers Service Corps joined the search yesterday and still has no news.

Police appealed yesterday, 9th August, for information, saying the 72-year-old left his home on Pek Tin Street in Tai Wai on Saturday morning and then went missing. He is about 1.73 metres tall, weighs about 65 kilograms, of medium build, with a square face, yellow complexion and short white hair.

When last seen he wore a blue top, black shorts and orange-and-white shoes and carried a black backpack. Anyone with news of him, or who has seen him, is asked to call 3661-1176 or 5562-1342, email the New Territories South Regional Missing Persons Investigation Team, or contact any police station.
