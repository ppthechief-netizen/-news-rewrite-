10th August 2026 - (Hong Kong) A knife attack struck Bel-Air in Pok Fu Lam today (10th August). A foreign man brandished a blade and wounded a foreign domestic helper, while a male security guard was hurt trying to stop him from continuing the assault. Officers arrested the man at the scene and took him back to a police station for investigation.

In further inquiries officers searched the suspect's flat and found a female cat with a head injury that they believe is linked to the case. The animal was hurt near the right ear; whether the wound was inflicted by a knife is not yet clear. Police handed the cat to the Society for the Prevention of Cruelty to Animals so that veterinarians can carry out further examination and treatment.

At about 9.24am today police received multiple reports that a man at Bel-Air, 550 Victoria Road, was waving a knife about 28 centimetres long and attacking people. Emergency Unit and Rapid Response Force officers put on stab-proof vests, raced to the estate and arrested the man on suspicion of wounding. The foreign domestic helper who was attacked sustained two knife wounds each about 2.5 centimetres long on the neck, plus another 2.5-centimetre knife wound on the back. The male security guard scraped his elbow while intervening to stop the man.

The case is provisionally classified as wounding. Officers are still investigating what drove the attack, and West District Crime Squad Team 2 has taken over the file. Preliminary inquiries indicate the suspect is a foreign man aged about 20 to 30 who was not wearing a top or shoes when the attack unfolded; police suspect he may be suffering from a mental illness.
