10th August 2026 - (Hong Kong) The government today launched its Woh Chai Shan — Bishop Hill — upgrade in Sham Shui Po with a two-stage clear-out of unlawful and unsafe structures, facilities and clutter: first from the Tong Yin Street hillside rest area foot to the summit, then a sealed sweep from the Berwick Street foot to the top.

The Home Affairs Department said on social media that upgrade packages of varying pace are expected to complete from late August through September. On religious articles now on the hill, the department said the government particularly respects faith practice and has invited religious leaders to help handle items properly, including solemn rites and relocating them to suitable places. Officials pledged ongoing talks with stakeholders so the dual upgrade of facilities and safety can deliver better, safer recreation for residents.
