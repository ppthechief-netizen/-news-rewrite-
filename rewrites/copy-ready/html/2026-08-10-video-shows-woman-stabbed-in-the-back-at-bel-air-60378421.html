10th August 2026 - (Hong Kong) A knife attack at Bel-Air in Pok Fu Lam left a foreign domestic helper and a male security guard wounded after a man with a blade struck. Reader video obtained by reporters captures the perilous moment when the knifeman swings at a passer-by on the estate road.

In the footage the knifeman paces the carriageway, closes step by step on a woman walking past, then thrusts the knife into her back. After she is stabbed she bolts toward the opposite side of the road and falls. As he moves in for another blow, an African man rushes out in time and kicks him away, while a security guard at the same moment uses a broom to keep him off the woman — narrowly stopping a worse tragedy.

The reader clip shows the knifeman lingering on the road when the woman happens by. He moves to attack; she edges backward; a security officer also shouts "Don't move!" He ignores the warning, raises the knife and closes in anyway. Panicked, she steps into the roadway to dodge; he immediately brings the blade down into her back. She runs hard with him in pursuit, but after crossing to the opposite carriageway she stumbles and falls to the ground.

He advances to strike again when the African man charges forward and kicks him clear, and another security officer promptly holds a broom between the two so he cannot attack again. Field information said the African man is a friend of the knifeman. The same Bel-Air assault also left the foreign domestic helper and the male security guard injured before police detained the knife suspect on the spot at the scene.
