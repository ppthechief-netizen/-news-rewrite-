11th August 2026 - (Hong Kong) A Japanese teenage girl was choked and robbed in a hotel room at 32-34 Nathan Road, Tsim Sha Tsui, after inviting a man she had met through messaging software to chat, police inquiries show.

She was travelling alone and checked into the hotel on Tuesday, 4th August. Yesterday she met the man online and asked him up to the room for a conversation. When he arrived he saw her place HK$15,000 in cash into the in-room safe before the attack began.

He then pulled a black plastic bag over her head, gripped her neck, took the cash from the safe and fled the building. She reported the assault; officers searched the vicinity but found no one linked to the case at the scene.

She felt pain in her neck but refused hospital examination. The file has been assigned to Yau Tsim District crime investigation team 3 for follow-up.

Police are hunting a Chinese man aged 30 to 40, about 1.75 metres tall and of medium build, last seen in a dark long-sleeve top, dark trousers, a white cap and a white mask.

Hotel-room robberies that start with an online invitation underline the risk when large sums of cash are displayed at the door before a stranger enters, investigators say. Closed-circuit television on guest floors and lifts will be a priority as detectives try to reconstruct how the suspect entered and left the premises.

Tourists who arrange meetings with people they know only through messaging apps are urged not to show valuables, keep cash out of sight until they are sure who is entering, and seek help immediately if a visitor becomes aggressive.

Foot patrols around Nathan Road hotels remain part of the early response while the wanted description is circulated to neighbouring districts, and anyone who saw a man matching that clothing near the address on the evening of the robbery is asked to contact police.
