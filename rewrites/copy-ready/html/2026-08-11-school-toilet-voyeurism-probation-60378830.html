11th August 2026 - (Hong Kong) An 18-year-old student who filmed a woman in a girls' toilet at a Tung Chung secondary school has been placed on a one-year probation order after admitting voyeurism, with a magistrate saying she would have jailed him had he not been so young.

Lau Chun-ting, a student, pleaded guilty earlier to one count of voyeurism in case WKCC3531/2026. He was charged with filming a woman identified as X on 22nd January 2026 in a sixth-floor girls' toilet near room 608 at Ling Liang Church E Wun Secondary School on Lantau in Tung Chung. The offence carries serious penalties, but the court focused on his age and background when deciding the sentence today.

Defence counsel told West Kowloon Court that a pre-sentence report recorded academic pressure alongside sexual curiosity as factors behind the offence and recommended a one-year probation order rather than immediate custody. Miss Chan Wai-man, presiding magistrate, said that but for the defendant's youth she would have imposed a prison term. Accepting the report's recommendation, she decided to give him one chance and sentenced him to probation for twelve months.

The charge sheet placed the offence on the sixth floor of Ling Liang Church E Wun Secondary School in Tung Chung, near room 608, and identified the victim only as woman X to protect her privacy. Lau had entered his guilty plea at an earlier hearing before the case returned today for sentence in WKCC3531/2026 at West Kowloon Court.

When passing sentence, Miss Chan Wai-man said she accepted the pre-sentence report and its recommendation of a twelve-month probation order. She told Lau that, had he not been so young, she would have imposed immediate imprisonment for filming a woman in a school toilet, but she would instead give him one opportunity to reform under supervision. Defence counsel had urged the court to follow the report, citing academic pressure together with sexual curiosity as the background to the offence.
