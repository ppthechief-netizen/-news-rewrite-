10th August 2026 - (Hong Kong) Television Broadcasts Limited said it has signed a heads of agreement with Gaw Capital Partners affiliate Triton Square Limited on a planned joint venture to supply advanced computing power for artificial intelligence uses in Hong Kong.

Ordinary voting shares in the venture are proposed at 51 per cent for TVB and 49 per cent for Gaw Capital. The company would plan, direct and fund new compute facilities inside the group's corporate campus at Tseung Kwan O Industrial Estate.

Inside those facilities, directly or through affiliates, the venture would buy, configure and run graphics processors, central processors and related kit, then sell AI-related advanced compute to outside clients and TVB group companies on a subscription model.

After the heads of agreement, both sides aim to sign formal documents in the second half of 2026 to form the company and start the project. Roll-out would be phased over several years, funded by up to HK$2 billion of equity from Gaw Capital, project bank financing and TVB internal resources.

Phase 1A is targeted to deliver about 10,000 PetaFLOPS of planned capacity from the fourth quarter of 2027. TVB said it is seeking approvals from relevant government bodies.

The partners are also talking with CLP Holdings about power for the proposed plant and with international and mainland equipment suppliers about GPUs and other hardware. Specialist teams are being assembled to drive development.

Separately, Gaw Capital may subscribe for new TVB shares, warrants or convertible notes on terms still to be agreed. Any such issue would need a formal joint-venture agreement to start the compute build, plus Listing Rule compliance including shareholder approval.

On 15th July, TVB and Gaw Capital signed a letter of intent with a leading global technology group on subscribing for advanced compute services. Technical and commercial talks continue, as do options to finance the equipment for that subscription offering, with formal agreements also targeted for the second half of 2026.

TVB framed the plan as part of its shift from a traditional broadcaster to a diversified digital media company, widening revenue as its own compute needs rise. It is exploring AI tools that could let outside creators use its large content library and lift production efficiency, hoping to lead AI-driven media creation in Hong Kong and the region. The heads of agreement and letter of intent are non-binding while definitive deals are finalised.
