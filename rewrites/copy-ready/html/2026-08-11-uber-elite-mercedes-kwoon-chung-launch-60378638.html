11th August 2026 - (Hong Kong) Uber and Kwoon Chung Bus Holdings on 10th August launched Uber Elite, a premium booked service using Mercedes-Benz S-Class and V-Class cars from Kwoon Chung's licensed hire-car fleet, aimed at business travellers and high-spec airport or hotel transfers.

The cars are neither white-plate private cars nor taxis; they carry valid hire-car permits. Ages run roughly three to ten years. Kwoon Chung Bus executive director Wong Cheuk-tim said the first phase puts several dozen vehicles on the road, with fleet size to follow demand.

All Elite drivers are full-time Kwoon Chung staff in uniform, able to use Cantonese, Putonghua and English, after six hours of training and an exam. Vehicles have safety and driving monitors, bottled water, multi-port charging, wet wipes and free Wi-Fi. Trips must be booked in the Uber app — no street hails or taxi ranks.

Uber Hong Kong general manager Chung Chi-ting said the aim is "First Class on the Ground". Unlike some luxury transfers that need three to four hours' notice, Elite starts with a two-hour minimum that the team may shorten after watching demand.

Fares are distance-based, roughly HK$780 to HK$1,300 depending on the route, in line with peer services. Promotional codes run to 31st August. Airport trips offer Meet and Greet with a name board and automatic wait extensions if flight details are entered and the plane is late.

Mr Chung said Hong Kong is the first Uber Elite market outside the US after Los Angeles and San Francisco, and the first in Asia-Pacific, pairing Uber's platform with Kwoon Chung's local fleet skill.

The partners already run Hong Kong–Macau point-to-point transfers launched in February, which Mr Wong said drew strong airport demand and helps Kwoon Chung reach individual travellers beyond corporate clients.

He called the timing right as ride-hailing habits mature and smart mobility grows, hoping Uber's network plus Kwoon Chung's fleet management will set a new local standard for premium booked travel.
