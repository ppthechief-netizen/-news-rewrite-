11th August 2026 - (Hong Kong) A 29-year-old man who dragged his girlfriend's corpse on a trolley through Hung Shui Kiu after beating her with a wooden stick was jailed for three years and nine months for manslaughter at the High Court.

Ng Ka-sing was convicted of manslaughtering Ip Tsz-ching, aged 30, in Hong Kong between 28th and 29th April 2022, and of preventing the lawful burial of her body. He had originally faced a murder charge in case HCCC340/2024. After a trial the jury returned a manslaughter verdict only, and before the hearing opened he had indicated he was willing to plead guilty to manslaughter. The Hon Mrs Justice Judianna Barnes called for reports and sentenced him on 10th August.

The couple had been living with Ip's sworn sister and the sister's husband, known as Ah Long, and others at a flat in Fu On Garden on Tin Ha Road in Hung Shui Kiu. Ng, about 1.5 metres tall, was seen at about 5.50am on 29th April 2022 pulling a trolley with a human foot protruding. A passer-by and a cleaner confirmed it was a foot and called police. Ng stayed at the scene. Officers found a woman's body wrapped in a quilt with her head wound in cling film. When a woman police officer questioned him, Ng answered calmly that the dead woman was his girlfriend and that he had struck her dead with a stick by accident.

Forensic pathologist Fu Ka-chung said that if Ip had been 1.59 metres tall she would have weighed 64 kilograms. She had multiple bruises and abrasions and a large bruise inside the back of her head. Corrosive burns covered about fifty-five per cent of her skin across the chest, abdomen, limbs and private parts, some of them severe. He said death was caused by head injury and extensive corrosive burns, followed by asphyxia, and that the burns did not look self-inflicted.

In a video interview Ng said the sworn sister claimed Ip had a "fat-to-death" condition and must be kept awake to lose weight, ordering him to hit her if she fell asleep. He said he assaulted her between about 10.00pm on 27th April and 1.30am the next day, and again between 3.00am and 5.30am. He also said Ip had poured drain cleaner on herself, complained of pain and asked why she had to endure it, wondering whether she would get through the night. Ng told her that if she skipped meals that day she would not have to be beaten. He said he found her unresponsive at about 7.00am on 28th April, tried first aid without success, and later hauled the body away on a trolley around 3.00am on 29th April intending to surrender at a police station. He claimed the body made an "ah" sound when he wrapped her head.

At trial his account shifted. He said Ah Long repeatedly raised the rent from five thousand to thirty thousand dollars after they moved in, forcing him to borrow from a finance company, beating him for breaking house rules, stopping him leaving for work and making him, a Christian, copy Buddhist scriptures. About a month before the killing the sworn sister ordered Ip to exercise, sleep little, drink only water and eat vegetables every two days. On 27th April she accused Ip of stealing food and blamed Ng for poor supervision, after which he again beat Ip's back with a stick. Ah Long then made the couple play guessing and pendulum games while striking their ankles with a mallet and applying drain-cleaner-soaked cotton buds in circles on their upper backs. Ng showed burn scars to the jury and said he snatched drain cleaner away when Ip poured it on herself. After further stick blows Ip's face turned pale and her lips whitened. Ah Long said she was dead. Ng said he hugged the body and apologised; Ah Long suggested dismemberment, but Ng could not face the blood and chose the trolley instead. He clarified that she made no sound when cling film was applied.

Three other housemates — warehouse worker Choi Chi-long, then 31, housewife Cheng Pui-man, 28, and warehouse worker Chan Wing-hong, 27 — were earlier charged with preventing lawful burial, but the prosecution withdrew those charges in late 2023.
