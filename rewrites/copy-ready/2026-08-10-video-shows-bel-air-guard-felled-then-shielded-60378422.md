10th August 2026 - (Hong Kong) A knife-wielding attack struck Bel-Air at 550 Victoria Road on Hong Kong Island this morning (10th August), leaving a foreign domestic helper, a male security guard and a cat injured; police arrested the male suspect on the spot. Social clips show the suspected attacker chasing a guard with a knife as many people flee at full tilt. One guard then falls; the suspect moves to strike, but a colleague who sees it rushes forward to block him and tries calming hand gestures, after which the attacker steps back — drawing praise online for the guards' courage.

Reporters also obtained reader footage in which a foreign woman walking past is stabbed in the back and falls; as the suspect tries to chase and cut again, an African man kicks him away and a security guard holds them apart with a broom, narrowly averting a second wounding.

On Threads a netizen uploaded a clip of a bare-chested, barefoot man with a knife suddenly accelerating uphill, rushing toward foreign passers-by, then turning on a sunglass-wearing estate guard. Forced against the car-park entrance wall, the guard faces a raised blade; he falls but still kicks hard to keep the knifeman off. Other security staff move in with calming gestures that achieve little, and both sides later enter the car-park mouth.

Further reader film shows the man with a sharp knife confronting a male estate guard on the roadway. The guard retreats slowly to keep distance while the knifeman shadows him step for step. He closes once but is shouted back by arriving colleagues. Three guards then box him in and match his steps; a minibus pulling up to discharge passengers is driven off by security, and foreign passers-by turn around and leave.

Another sequence shows him pacing the road as a woman walks past. He moves to attack; she edges back; a guard shouts "Don't move!" He ignores the order, raises the knife and closes in. She panics into the carriageway; he stabs her back and chases as she runs. After she crosses and falls, he advances again until the African man — said by field sources to be his friend — kicks him clear and another guard holds a broom between them, stopping a second strike.
