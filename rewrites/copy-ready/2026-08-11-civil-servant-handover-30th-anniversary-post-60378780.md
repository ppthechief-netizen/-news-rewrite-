11th August 2026 - (Hong Kong) Hong Kong has begun early work on the 30th anniversary of the city's return to Chinese sovereignty in July 2027, elevating a civil service deputy secretary to head a new project planning office within the Home and Youth Affairs Bureau.

Miss Zhang Yi, JP, has moved from her D4 post as Deputy Secretary for the Civil Service to a D6 grade to run the anniversary unit, according to political sources. The Home and Youth Affairs Bureau recently established the Project Planning Office with her as director. Only a month after this year's 29th anniversary, officials are setting up a steering committee to guide strategy and coordination, plus dedicated task groups to plan large celebrations, community programmes and promotional activities in major mainland and overseas cities.

Planning remains at an early conceptual stage. Policy bureaux are being asked to propose activities within their portfolios, and the simplest route may be to rebrand last year's 29th anniversary programmes for the 30th milestone. Last week the Education Bureau issued a circular to primary, secondary and special school principals outlining the national education calendar for the new academic year. It said schools may hold celebrations on or around Handover Day, including flag-raising and anthem ceremonies, mural projects and greeting-card design to mark three decades since the establishment of the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region.

Celebrations are expected to extend well beyond campus ceremonies. For the 25th anniversary in 2022, then chief secretary John Lee unveiled more than one hundred events, including major exhibitions of Dunhuang cave treasures, rare prehistoric dinosaur fossils and Baroque paintings from Italy's Museo di Capodimonte. Officials will also design an official 30th anniversary logo. Past practice suggests the programme could be announced about seven months ahead, meaning details might emerge as early as December this year.

Political circles widely expect President Xi Jinping to visit Hong Kong as he did for the 25th anniversary, when he swore in John Lee's government. Miss Zhang is said to have taken up the director's post before August, and an eight-strong team is now in place. She has one deputy director, one assistant director, four executive officer-grade staff and one general manager. Among them is her former colleague Chan Sun-hei, who retired as head of the Civil Service Bureau's general grades but returned to help. Chan served the government for nearly forty years as an executive officer and head of the executive officer and clerical grades, and is best known for coordinating mass Covid testing during the pandemic.

Sources describe the anniversary assignment as a mark of senior confidence in Miss Zhang. If she performs well, completing the D6 posting could at least secure a directorate-grade department head role. Gordon Leung Chung-tai, who led the 20th anniversary celebrations coordination office, later became Postmaster General and Director of Social Welfare. However, many past anniversary office heads climbed further. Michael Mak Tai-wai, who ran the 25th anniversary project planning office, later became Permanent Secretary for Innovation, Technology and Industry. Stephen Lam Nal-moon, who headed the 10th anniversary preparatory office in 2007, was promoted the same year to Permanent Secretary for Development (Planning and Lands) and later served as Permanent Secretary for the Civil Service (Special Duties) and Permanent Secretary for Home Affairs. Officials say Miss Zhang enjoys a strong reputation for competence and could follow a similar path if the 30th anniversary programme succeeds.
