11th August 2026 - (Hong Kong) A large overnight power cut left thousands of households in Yuen Long without electricity after an underground cable fault, with residents at Shui Pin Wai Estate reporting a blackout from the early hours and CLP Power Hong Kong aiming to restore supply by about 10am.

At around 4am today, residents reported hearing a loud explosion-like sound from Wu Shui House and called the police. Officers and technicians who attended found the noise was a misunderstanding rather than a blast, but the building did have an electrical fault and some blocks lost power. Online posts soon showed the wider estate was also dark from around that time, with air-conditioners and fans dead and many people waking in the heat.

Among the seven residential blocks, only Hong Shui House kept a supply. Elsewhere lifts were out of service and residents said the estate had effectively plunged into a full blackout. A reporter on site found CLP crews already carrying out emergency repairs, with every block except Hong Shui House still without power.

CLP told reporters that at 4.22am an underground cable fault in the Yuen Long area affected electricity for Shui Pin Wai Estate and nearby customers, about 2,400 accounts in total. Engineers were working flat out, deploying temporary generators and laying temporary cables to restore power as quickly as possible, with repair work expected to finish around 10am.

The utility also said its community support team was on hand to help, providing portable batteries as emergency backup for affected residential care homes, and was staying in close contact with the local Home Affairs Department office and district councillors while the outage continued.

For households stuck without cooling in the humid summer night, the blackout meant more than inconvenience: with lifts stopped, elderly residents and those living higher up faced particular difficulty moving in and out of their blocks. Estate common areas and stairwells stayed dim as crews worked through the remaining hours before the target restoration window.

CLP's reply stressed that temporary generation and cable rerouting were both part of the recovery plan, underlining how quickly the fault cascaded across the estate and neighbouring customers once the underground circuit failed. Until supply returns, residents have been left relying on battery packs, open windows and whatever residual charge remains on phones and fans.
