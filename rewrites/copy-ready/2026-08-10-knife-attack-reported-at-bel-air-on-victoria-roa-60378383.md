10th August 2026 - (Hong Kong) This morning (10th August) a man brandished a knife and attacked people at Bel-Air, 550 Victoria Road on Hong Kong Island, wounding a foreign domestic helper and a male security guard. Emergency Unit and Rapid Response Force officers in stab-proof vests raced to the scene and arrested the male suspect on the spot.

A clip circulating on social platforms captures the suspected attacker chasing a security guard with a knife as multiple people flee at a run. One guard then loses his footing and falls; the suspect moves in to strike, and the fallen guard can only kick out to keep him back — a highly dangerous sequence that left viewers describing the confrontation as extremely perilous.

On Threads, a netizen uploaded footage showing a bare-chested man gripping a knife suddenly accelerate uphill, rush toward foreign passers-by, then turn and close on an estate security guard wearing sunglasses. The guard is forced against the wall at the car-park entrance; the knifeman raises the blade at one point. The guard falls but still fights for space, kicking to stop the attacker coming closer. Other security staff then step in and try to calm the knifeman with hand gestures, but to little effect, and both sides later move into the car-park entrance.

Separately, readers supplied footage obtained by reporters showing the man holding a sharp knife and confronting a male estate guard on the roadway. The guard retreats slowly, keeping distance, but the knifeman matches his steps and stays close. He closes in once, only to be shouted back by security staff who arrive next. Three guards then surround him and move with his pace; a minibus that pulls up ready to set down passengers is waved away by security, and foreign passers-by who reach the spot turn around and leave the scene.
