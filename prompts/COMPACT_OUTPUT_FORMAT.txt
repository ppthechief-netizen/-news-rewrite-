Elaborated multi-paragraph house format (STRICT).

## Length (STRICT — every rewrite)
- Body must be **at least 300 words** (count dateline through last body paragraph only).
- Prefer **350–450+ words**; longer HK01 sources often need **500–900+**.
- **Forbidden:** one-paragraph summaries, two-paragraph stubs, or any body under 300 words.
- Soft guide ~**90–110%** of source when that exceeds the floor (fuller preferred). Cover every material point; never cut facts for brevity.

## Paragraphs (STRICT — every rewrite)
- Body paragraphs MUST be separated by a **blank line** (standard Markdown).
- Single newlines alone are FORBIDDEN in the body: they render as one wall of text.
- Minimum **5** body paragraphs; **6–10+** when the source has several themes.
- Prefer short-to-medium wire paragraphs (often 2–5 sentences), one beat per paragraph — not mega-blocks and not a single digest block.
- Canonical layout: see `.cursor/rules/markdown-style.mdc`.

## Coverage (STRICT — every rewrite)
- Cover EVERY material point in the source paragraphs: who/what/when/where/why, all figures, places, process steps, reactions, caveats, secondary context, and named quotes (each quote ≤25 words).
- Do NOT summarise away detail. Walk the source end-to-end; if a fact, number, place, name, or reaction is in the notes, it must appear in the English body.
- Never cut facts to hit a short word count; expand by elaborating source material, not by inventing.

## Footer source link (STRICT — every rewrite)
- After the Footer SEO/meta line, always end with an HK01 link on its own line:
  - Exclusive: `Credit: HK01 — original reporting. Source: [HK01](<article_url>)`
  - Non-exclusive: `Source: [HK01](<article_url>)`
- Use the real article URL from inbox JSON (prefer `https://www.hk01.com/article/<id>`).
