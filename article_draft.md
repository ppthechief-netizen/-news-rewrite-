11th August 2026 - (Hong Kong) Michelle Wai has made her first public appearance since a glass shard sliced her upper lip for more than 50 stitches, turning up at Beijing's Hundred Flowers Awards with a discreet plaster that barely dented her red-carpet polish.

The Hong Kong actress was hurt while shooting the drama Lies Hunter when broken glass cut her left upper lip. The wound ran through skin and vermilion, severed a blood vessel and tore muscle. Bleeding was heavy and the injury was classed as a full-thickness laceration, so she needed immediate suturing — more than 50 stitches closing the layers that form a working mouth.

She was seen at the Baihua ceremony with tape still covering the site. Recovery looks well advanced: colour, posture and overall appearance were intact enough that fans need not panic about a lasting collapse of her screen look. She also wore signature Chaumet Josephine jewellery, a graceful counterpoint to the medical strip on her mouth.

Alongside the photographs she recorded a brief update. She is fully back at work, she said, but the wound is not completely healed. Stitches came out only recently, so for the next few days she cannot put makeup on her face, especially over the cut. She is watching her diet, avoiding dark-coloured foods as much as possible. Ordinary speech is fine; big facial movements and loud laughter are not. "There will definitely be a little scar," she added, "but I will slowly do different treatments — luckily my husband can help follow up."

That husband is aesthetic doctor Kenley Chau, who earlier explained how glass fragments had to be lifted out and why so many sutures were required on both the inner and outer lip. Wai's return to a mainland awards night so soon after destitching underlines how quickly she has pushed back into public life — plaster and dietary bans included — while still rationing smiles and foundation until the tissue finishes knitting.

For an actress whose face is her instrument, the Hundred Flowers turn was as much a medical bulletin as a fashion stop: stitches out, plaster on, no full makeup, no roaring laughs — and yet, by the look of the carpet shots, the "state is excellent" verdict her fans wanted to hear.
