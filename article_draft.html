10th August 2026 - (Hong Kong) Officers who later searched the Bel-Air flat of a man arrested over this morning's knife attack found a female cat with a wound near its right ear — whether from a blade still unclear — and handed the animal to the Society for the Prevention of Cruelty to Animals for veterinary checks, believing the injury may be linked to the case.

Multiple callers rang police at about 9.24am reporting a man brandishing a knife about 28 centimetres long at Bel-Air, 550 Victoria Road, Pok Fu Lam. Emergency Unit and Rapid Response officers in stab-proof vests detained a foreign man aged about 20 to 30 on suspicion of wounding; he was bare-chested and barefoot when seized and, after preliminary inquiries, officers suspect he may have been suffering from a mental illness. A foreign domestic helper sustained two knife wounds each about 2.5 centimetres long on the neck and another 2.5-centimetre wound on the back; a male security guard scraped his elbow while trying to stop the assault. The case is listed as wounding, motive remains under investigation, and West District Crime Squad Team 2 has taken over.
